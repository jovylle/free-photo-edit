Is it really free? How many edits do I get?

Yes. You get 3 free edits, no payment needed, tied to the browser you use. Packs of 10, 25 or 60 edits are sold on the home page when they are available.

The free service is as-is, and the limits may change.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign up and we do not ask for your name. An anonymous ID in your browser holds your edits and history. The only time we ask for an email is when you buy a pack of edits.

Clear your browser data and that ID, your balance and your history are gone.

Can I write in Tagalog or Bisaya?

Yes. Type in English, Tagalog, or Bisaya, the way you would say it out loud.

Short and specific is easier to follow than a long paragraph.

What photos can I upload?

1 to 5 photos per edit. Each must be JPEG, PNG, or WebP and 5 MB or smaller, and the instruction box takes up to 2000 characters.

You get back one PNG, up to 1024 by 1024 pixels.

How long does one edit take?

There is no fixed wait. The page checks for your result every 10 seconds and shows it as soon as it is ready.

You can leave the page. The edit keeps running, and it shows in your history.

What happens if the edit fails?

The job is marked failed and your edit is not used up, so you can try again.

A different photo or a shorter instruction may get past it.

Are my photos private? How long do you keep them?

Your photo goes to OpenAI to make the edit. We do not sell your photos or personal data, and we do not use them to train models. Files are deleted 7 days after the edit finishes or fails.

A job link is not a password: anyone with it can open the result. Cleanup runs hourly, and a Cloudflare Turnstile check may appear before an edit.

Can I use the result for work or sell it?

The terms do not say we own your photo, and they do not give you rights over the result either. Check the result before paid or official use.

Only upload photos you took or have permission to use.

Why does it say the server is busy?

Too many edits are running at once, or your network sent too many in the last minute. Wait a minute and try again.

Free runs also cap at 24 per network, 12 per device and 60 for the whole service each day.

Does it work on my phone or in the Facebook browser?

The page is built for phones and we test it at phone screen sizes. We have not tested the Facebook or Messenger in-app browser, so if a button does nothing, open the link in Chrome or Safari.

Free edits stay with the browser you start in.
