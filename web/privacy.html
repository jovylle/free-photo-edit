Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

What we handle

Your photo and the instruction you type. The photo is sent to an AI image provider (OpenAI) to make the edit.

Your results and history. Uploads and edited images are stored on our server so your history and results can load again after you reload the page.

An anonymous ID saved in your browser. It keeps your history and credits together. It is not your name or account.

Your IP address, used only to limit how many free edits one network can run.

Bot check. When it is turned on, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a real person is using the page.

What we do not do

We do not sell your photos or personal data, and we do not use them to train models.

How long we keep things

Photos and results are kept for a limited time, then old files are deleted.

Your choices

Clear your browser's site data to remove the saved ID and form. Do not upload photos you do not have the right to use.
