Free Photo Edit

About

Free Photo Edit lets you change one photo for free using a short instruction in plain words. Pick a suggestion like Background, Clothes, Restore, or Custom, or type your own (English, Tagalog, or Bisaya).

How it works

Upload a photo, describe the change, then tap Edit. An AI image model makes the edit and the result appears under your photo, ready to download.

Why it has limits

Every edit costs real money in AI fees. Free runs are limited per person and per day so the service stays available for everyone.

Support it

If the free edits run out, you can buy a pack of edits (10, 25 or 60) on the home page, paid with GCash, Maya, GrabPay or card through Xendit. You can also donate by scanning the GCash QR on the home page or through Ko-fi. Both cover the AI costs. Donations are voluntary.
